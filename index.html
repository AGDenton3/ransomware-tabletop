How it works

≈15 min
  1. Open a case fileEach organization has its own systems, backups, insurance and legal duties. The org profile is always one click away.
  2. Read the inboxEmails, calls, texts and the attacker's portal arrive as the clock moves. Some bring facts. Some bring pressure.
  3. Make the callClick or press 1–4. Five meters react: Cost, Downtime, Data Loss, Public Trust, Legal Exposure.
  4. Face the debriefA stamped grade, every decision mapped to NIST CSF 2.0, what you missed, and what preparation would have changed.
