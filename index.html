CSCE 477 · Incident response exercise

It's 7:42 a.m. The screens say your files are encrypted.

You are the IT lead at a small organization with no security team, and a ransom note has just appeared. Every call you make costs money, time, data or trust, and the clock keeps moving whether you decide or not. At the end, a debrief maps each of your decisions to the NIST incident-response framework.

How to play

Four steps, about fifteen minutes

  1. Pick an organizationEach scenario has its own systems, backups, insurance and legal duties. Open the org profile any time.
  2. Read the inboxEmails, calls and texts arrive as the clock moves. Some bring facts; some bring pressure.
  3. Make the callChoose with the mouse or keys 1–4. Watch the five meters: Cost, Downtime, Data Loss, Public Trust and Legal Exposure.
  4. Read the debriefSee your grade, every decision tagged by NIST CSF 2.0 Function, what you missed, and what preparation would have changed.
